Privacy Policy

Nankana Cricket Play-On and Chai Cafe ("we") runs the booking app for our cricket play area, cafe, coffee shop and kids area.

What we collect

Your name, mobile number and booking details (date, hours, advance payment reference). The app also creates an anonymous ID on your phone so you can see your own bookings.

Your PIN

If you set a PIN, it is stored securely by Google Firebase Authentication. We cannot see it. If you forget it, message us and we will help you set a new one.

Why

To reserve your slot, confirm your advance payment, contact you about your booking, and prevent fake bookings.

Where it is stored and who sees it

Your data is stored with Google Firebase. Only you and our staff can see your booking details. Other players only see that a time slot is taken. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Payments

Advance payments are made in your JazzCash or Easypaisa app (or the JazzCash payment page). We never see your wallet PIN or card details.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete your data, message us on WhatsApp using the number shown in the app, and we will act on your request.

Children

The app is for adults booking on behalf of players and children.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new version here.
